How the numbers work

Win odds are a local Monte Carlo estimate: unknown cards are shuffled, opponent hands are dealt, and the best five-card Hold'em hand wins each trial. Draw odds enumerate all remaining runouts exactly.

Reference material: PokerNews odds calculator (street-by-street odds) and Super/System overview (Brunson's approach).
